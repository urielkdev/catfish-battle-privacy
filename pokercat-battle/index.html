PokerCat Battle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy describes the Android version of PokerCat Battle, package com.pokercat.battle, published by LSTT (Uriel Kindermann Caminha). Privacy questions can be sent to lstt.publisher@gmail.com.

Information handled by the game

PokerCat Battle is a single-player game. It does not require a game account or ask for your name, email address, or other personal information to play. The game does not send your gameplay or device information to the developer or to an analytics or advertising service.

Your progress and preferences are saved on your device. This includes unlocked stages, experience, in-game currency, equipment, skills, achievements, Tarot cards, daily reward dates, language, audio levels, and automatic play settings. These records let you resume your adventure and keep your chosen settings.

The game reads your device's language setting to choose an initial language and uses the device's date for daily rewards. This information is processed locally and is not transmitted by the game.

Permissions, advertising, and tracking

The Android game does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier. It contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases and Google Play

Purchasing, downloading, and updating the game through Google Play are handled by Google. The game does not receive your payment card details. Google's handling of your account, purchase, and store activity is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. Google Play may provide the developer with sales reports, aggregated usage statistics, and technical reports through Play Console. These are separate from data transmitted by the game, which has no reporting integration.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to reply, investigate the reported problem, and provide support. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information.

Support correspondence is handled through our email provider and retained only as needed to resolve your request, maintain necessary support records, or meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell your support information or use it for advertising. You may contact us to request access to or deletion of your support correspondence, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Storage and deletion

Gameplay saves remain in the app's storage on your device until they are replaced or deleted. The game does not upload saves to a developer-operated server or provide account-based cloud synchronization. Android backup is disabled for this app.

You can delete local game data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. This deletes your local progress and preferences. The developer cannot retrieve a deleted local save. Purchase records maintained by Google are handled separately under Google's policies.

Policy updates

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the date above will be revised. Questions about this policy or your support information can be sent to lstt.publisher@gmail.com.
