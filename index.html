Privacy policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Catfish Battle is provided by LSTT. Privacy contact: lstt.publisher@gmail.com.

The current version is an offline single-player game. The app does not collect or transmit personal data, use analytics or advertising SDKs, display advertisements, or offer purchases with real money. No account or login is required. The Android export does not request Internet permission.

Game progress, achievements, language, audio settings and other preferences are stored locally on your device. They are used to run the game and resume your campaign. The developer does not receive these files or share them with third parties. Automatic Android backup is disabled for this app. Local data remains until you delete it, clear the app's storage through Android settings, or uninstall the game. Deleting local data also deletes your saved progress.

If you contact support by email, the developer receives the email address and information you choose to send. These messages are used to respond to your request and resolve the reported issue. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. You can contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable recordkeeping obligations. This support correspondence is separate from data collected by the game.

Google Play and Android may handle information under their own privacy policies when you download or use their services. This policy describes the game itself.

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the Google Play disclosures will be updated before releasing the affected version.

Vigência: 28 de setembro de 2026

Catfish Battle é disponibilizado por LSTT. Contato para privacidade: lstt.publisher@gmail.com.
